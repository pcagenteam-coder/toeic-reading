## 一招
> 先扫题干，圈出人名、数字、日期  
> 带着锚点回原文，只读附近句  
> 对照同义改写，排除无关段落  

## 适用信号
- 题干出现具体人名、金额、日期或编号
- 原文较长且信息分散在多段
- 选项含相近数字、时间或条件

## 示范题
### 示范（HB08-0 · 进阶 · 参考 60 秒）

**[email] Halvorsen Facilities: Nordvik Visit Update**

> Hello all,
> The client group from Nordvik will now arrive at 10:40 a.m. on Thursday, 14 March, not 9:30 as first planned. Their train was rescheduled because of track maintenance. Please have Conference Room B set up by 10:15 with eight chairs, a projector, and two water pitchers. The reception desk should print visitor badges for six guests; two colleagues from Nordvik's legal office will join remotely. Lunch will be served in the atrium at 12:30, so do not order the usual sandwich platters. Instead, Marisol in Procurement will arrange a hot buffet. If the projector cannot be repaired by Wednesday afternoon, notify me immediately and we will use the portable screen from the training room.
> Thanks,
> Priya

**According to the email, what should the facilities team do if the projector is still not working by Wednesday afternoon?**

- (A) Ask Marisol to find another projector.
- (B) Print extra visitor badges for the Nordvik group.
- (C) Contact Priya without delay.
- (D) Move the client meeting to the training room.

**三步拆解**
1. 第1步：题干出现时间 Wednesday afternoon 和物品 projector，先圈定为定位锚点。
2. 第2步：回原文只扫含 projector 的末句，看到 Wednesday afternoon 与 notify me immediately。
3. 第3步：确认 me 指发件人 Priya；地点、采购、访客证等选项均不匹配，排除后选 C。

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 C**　题干关键词是 projector 和 Wednesday afternoon。原文末尾的 If the projector cannot be repaired by Wednesday afternoon, notify me immediately 直接对应“立刻联系我”。发件人为 Priya，因此选 C。其余选项把便携屏幕、自助餐负责人和访客证任务混入，均无依据。

- 排除 A：Marisol 负责安排热自助餐，与投影仪维修无关。
- 排除 B：打印访客证是接待处的常规任务，不是投影仪故障时的指示。
- 排除 D：原文只说可从培训室取便携屏幕，并非把会议移到培训室。

</details>

### 练习 1（HB08-1 · 基础 · 参考 45 秒）

**[email] Your Building Access Card**

> From: Nadia Feld, Facilities Coordinator
> To: All Halvorsen Staff
> Date: March 4
> Subject: New Building Access Cards
> 
> Dear colleagues,
> 
> Our current access cards will stop working on March 31. New cards are ready for pickup at the front desk on the first floor.
> 
> Please bring your employee ID when you come. You can pick up your card between 8:00 A.M. and 5:00 P.M., Monday to Friday. If you work in the Riverside office, your card will be mailed to you instead.
> 
> After you receive your new card, please test it at the main entrance. If it does not open the door, contact the facilities team at extension 220.
> 
> Thank you,
> Nadia Feld

**According to the email, what should staff bring when they pick up their new cards?**

- (A) A copy of the email
- (B) Their employee ID
- (C) A signed form
- (D) Their old access card

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 B**　题干关键词是 bring 和 pick up，回原文定位到“Please bring your employee ID when you come.”，直接对应选项 B。原文只要求带员工证件，没有提到邮件副本、旧卡或签字表格，因此其余选项均无依据。

- 排除 A：原文未要求携带邮件副本。
- 排除 C：原文没有提到任何需要签字的表格。
- 排除 D：旧卡将于 3 月 31 日失效，但领取新卡时并未要求交回。

</details>

**[email] Order #4471 — Partial Delivery**

> To: Ingrid Salvesen, Office Manager, Halvorsen

> Date: March 4
> Subject: Order #4471 — Partial Delivery
> 
> Dear Ms. Salvesen,
> 
> Thank you for Halvorsen's order of 40 ergonomic desk chairs for the Trondheim branch. Unfortunately, the graphite model is out of stock until late March. We can ship 25 units in charcoal gray on March 12, which is the closest color currently available.
> 
> The remaining 15 graphite chairs would arrive on April 2. If the color change is acceptable, please confirm by March 8 so that we can hold the March 12 shipment. Otherwise, we will send the complete order in April.
> 
> Please note that the assembly service you requested is included at no extra charge for orders of more than 30 units.
> 
> Regards,
> Marcus Teale
> Bergen Office Supply

**According to the email, what is Ms. Salvesen asked to do by March 8?**

- (A) Change the delivery date to April 2
- (B) Approve an additional fee for assembly service
- (C) Reduce the order from 40 chairs to 25
- (D) Confirm that charcoal gray chairs are acceptable

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 D**　以题干中的日期 March 8 和人名 Ms. Salvesen 为锚点，定位到「If the color change is acceptable, please confirm by March 8」。前一句说明可先发 25 把 charcoal gray（炭灰色）椅子，因此要她确认的就是能否接受这一颜色替换，D 为同义改写。

- 排除 A：4 月 2 日是剩余 15 把椅子的预计到货日，并非要求她更改送货日期。
- 排除 B：原文说组装服务对超过 30 台的订单免费提供，并不需要额外付费批准。
- 排除 C：25 把是 3 月 12 日可先发货的数量，邮件并未要求她削减订单。

</details>

**[email] Visitor registration changes from 14 March**

> From: Priya Raman, Office Services
> To: All Halvorsen Staff
> Subject: Visitor registration changes from 14 March
> 
> Starting 14 March, employees must register every visitor to our Oslo office through the online guest portal at least one business day before arrival. Please enter the visitor's full name and company, then forward the confirmation email to reception@halvorsen.com. Visitors without a confirmation will wait in the lobby while reception contacts the host. For meetings with more than eight guests, email Marta Eide in Office Services at least two business days before the meeting and no later than 10 a.m. Badges must be worn in all common areas and returned to the front desk when visitors leave. The paper sign-in sheet will be discontinued after 21 March. For help, call extension 4421.

**According to the email, what should employees do for a meeting with more than eight guests?**

- (A) Register the guests at the front desk on the day of the meeting.
- (B) Ask reception to prepare paper sign-in sheets.
- (C) Send each guest's confirmation email to Marta Eide.
- (D) Contact Marta Eide at least two business days in advance.

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 D**　题干关键词 “more than eight guests” 可定位到原文 “For meetings with more than eight guests, email Marta Eide ... at least two business days before the meeting and no later than 10 a.m.”，因此应至少提前两个工作日联系 Marta Eide。其余选项分别混淆了确认邮件的收件人、访客登记方式和即将停用的纸质签到表。

- 排除 A：访客应至少提前一个工作日通过在线访客系统登记，而非当天到前台登记。
- 排除 B：纸质签到表将在 3 月 21 日后停用，不能再要求前台准备。
- 排除 C：原文要求将确认邮件转发给 reception@halvorsen.com，而不是 Marta Eide。

</details>

### 练习 4（HB08-4 · 熟练 · 参考 75 秒）

**[email] Arrangements for the Solvang Audit Visit**

> From: Elena Markovic, Office Manager
> To: Halvorsen Staff
> Subject: Arrangements for the Solvang Audit Visit
> 
> The Solvang audit team will visit our Oslo office on Thursday, 12 September. Their flight lands at 08:15, and a shuttle will bring them to reception by 09:00. Please note that the main conference room will be unavailable from 09:00 to 11:30 for their briefing. Staff who usually book that room should use Meeting Room 4B instead; its key can be collected from reception. At 11:30, Inger Solberg from Finance will give the visitors a short overview of our compliance procedures in the training room. A buffet lunch will follow in the cafeteria at 12:45. If you have documents for the auditors, place them in the labeled tray at reception by 16:00 on 11 September. Do not deliver them directly to the conference room.

**According to the email, what should staff do by 16:00 on 11 September?**

- (A) Give documents to Inger Solberg in the training room.
- (B) Place documents for the auditors in the labeled tray at reception.
- (C) Leave documents in Meeting Room 4B.
- (D) Deliver documents directly to the main conference room.

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 B**　题干中的日期“16:00 on 11 September”是定位锚点，回原文可找到“place them in the labeled tray at reception by 16:00 on 11 September”。其中 them 指代前一句的“documents for the auditors”，因此应把文件放到接待处的标记托盘。Meeting Room 4A 是给原会议室使用者改用的房间，Inger Solberg 是做合规流程简介，均与交文件无关。

- 排除 A：Inger Solberg 在培训室做合规流程简介，并不是收文件的人。
- 排除 C：Meeting Room 4A 是供原会议室使用者改用的房间，文中未说在那儿放审计文件。
- 排除 D：文中明确说不要把文件直接送到会议室，与正确做法相反。

</details>

### 练习 5（HB08-5 · 真考+ · 参考 90 秒）

**[email] Advanced Negotiation Workshop — revised arrangements**

> To: All Halvorsen staff enrolled in the Advanced Negotiation Workshop
> From: Priya Raghunathan, Learning & Development
> Date: 3 March
> 
> Thank you for confirming your places in the workshop led by Ingrid Solberg. Because demand from the Oslo and Trondheim offices exceeded our original estimate, the session has been moved from the Bergen Room to the Aurora Suite, which seats 40 rather than 30. The date is unchanged: 14 March, 09:00–16:30.
> 
> Two practical points. First, the pre-work case study must reach me by 10 March. Anyone who has not submitted it will lose their place to a colleague on the waiting list, since we cannot admit unprepared participants. Second, the caterer needs final numbers, so let me know by 7 March if you require a vegetarian or gluten-free lunch. Parking permits are no longer issued at reception; collect yours from the security desk on the day.

**According to the email, what will happen to an enrollee who does not hand in the case study on time?**

- (A) They will be asked to confirm their meal requirements by 7 March.
- (B) Their place will be offered to a colleague on the waiting list.
- (C) They will be allowed to attend if they complete it during the lunch break.
- (D) They will be moved to a later session held in the Bergen Room.

<details><summary>答案与解析</summary>

**答案 B**　题干问“未按时交案例研究的人会怎样”，锚点是日期 10 March 和 pre-work case study，回到原文第二段：“Anyone who has not submitted it will lose their place to a colleague on the waiting list”，即名额转给候补同事，故选 B。原文只说“cannot admit unprepared participants”，并未允许午休补做（C错）；房间更换（Bergen→Aurora）是针对全体学员，且日期未变，D 无依据；7 March 的餐食确认是另一项独立要求，A 是拿另一个日期设的干扰。

- 排除 A：7 March 是餐食特殊需求的确认期限，与案例研究无关。
- 排除 C：原文只说不能接收未做准备者，从未允许午休时间补做。
- 排除 D：房间由 Bergen 改为 Aurora，且日期未变，不存在“改到之后场次”。

</details>
